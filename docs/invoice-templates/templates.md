# Invoice Template Designs

- structured_gst_grid: black-grid operational document with a bill/ship split, rate-wise GST table, totals grid, and declaration.
- premium_elegant: framed, spacious layout with restrained accent styling, customer cards, and a signature panel.
- compact_detailed_gst: dense product and tax presentation for high-line-count documents.
- modern_split_panel: supplier-led header, invoice status panel, customer cards, and shaded totals area.
- executive_corporate_gst: corporate header, reference block, detailed customer sections, GST summary, payable panel, seal, and signature area.

Every layout uses stored CRM invoice and item snapshots. Shared partials reduce repeated GST, totals, payment-QR, and line-item markup, while each top-level layout has independent structure and print CSS.
